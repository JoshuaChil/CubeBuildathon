## Story outline (draft)

1. **Problem:** NYC roads are degrading, and there isn't enough money to fix everything. Cities lack fresh, street-level data on where the damage is.
2. **Insight:** every bike and car already feels the road. A $X sensor box can turn every ride into a road survey.
3. **Demo:** a live ride or a recording → bumps appear on the map → ranked repair list.
4. **Impact:** fix the roads that are **rough and busy** first, so limited funding helps the most people.
5. **Scale:** mentor feedback says to focus on roads and cars. Possible paths: delivery fleets, rideshare, city vehicles, bike-share.
6. **Ask / next steps.**
