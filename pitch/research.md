# Problem research & framing

## Starting points (from team notes)

- NYC infrastructure is degrading, and there isn't enough funding to address it.
- Look into government funding, and whether this data can lead to measurable impact.
- Two things to measure: the **busyness** of roads and the **rating** (quality) of roads.
- The output is a **to-do list for the government**: which roads are the busiest *and* in the worst shape?
- Mentor (Derek): the hardware is good. Focus on **applications in roads, including cars**.
